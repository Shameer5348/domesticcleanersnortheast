Cleaning prices

Clear pricing for regular cleaning, and an accurate quote for deep cleans and end of tenancy cleans.

Regular cleaning: from £40 an hour

Most homes need two to three hours a visit, weekly or fortnightly. We'll confirm exactly how many hours your home needs and agree a regular price with you before your first clean. No contract — pause or cancel whenever you need to.

Deep cleaning and end of tenancy cleaning

These are priced individually, because no two homes are the same. Rather than give you a generic online estimate that changes once we see the property, we ask a few quick questions and come back with an accurate price. Factors that affect the price include:

  • Size of the propertyNumber of bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • Current conditionHow long since the home was last deep cleaned.
  • ExtrasInside the oven, fridge, windows or carpets, if needed.
  • Access and timingHow soon you need the clean completed.

Tell us about your home using the quote form and we'll come back with a price, usually the same day.

Common questions

Is there a minimum booking?

Regular cleans are usually booked for a minimum of two hours a visit, so your cleaner has enough time to do the job properly.

Do you charge a callout fee?

No. You only pay for the cleaning itself.

How do I pay?

We'll confirm payment details when we agree your price. This is covered before your first visit, so there are no surprises.
