Domestic cleaning services

Whether you want a cleaner every week or one thorough clean before you move, we’ll send someone who knows what they’re doing.

  • Regular house cleaning

    Weekly or fortnightly cleans of your kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas, so your home stays on top without you having to think about it.

    For busy households who want the cleaning off their list for good.

  • Deep cleaning

    A top-to-bottom clean that reaches the places a regular clean doesn’t: inside cupboards, behind appliances, skirting boards, grout and limescale.

    For spring cleans, homes that need catching up, or a fresh start before regular cleans begin.

  • End of tenancy cleaning

    A full clean of the property when you move out or move in, so it’s ready for the inventory check or the next tenant.

    For tenants, landlords and letting agents.

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?

Newcastle, Sunderland, Gateshead, Gosforth, Jesmond, Ponteland, Whitley Bay, Tynemouth, North Shields, Cramlington and Morpeth. If your area isn’t listed, send us your postcode and we’ll let you know.

How soon can you start?

We have experienced cleaners available now. Send us your postcode and the type of clean you need, and we’ll give you our earliest start date.

What’s the difference between a regular clean and a deep clean?

A regular clean keeps a home in good order: surfaces, floors, kitchen and bathrooms. A deep clean goes further, reaching inside cupboards, behind appliances and into the build-up a regular clean doesn’t cover. Many clients start with a deep clean, then move to regular cleans.

Do you clean for landlords and letting agents?

Yes. We carry out end of tenancy cleans for tenants, landlords and letting agents across the areas we cover.
