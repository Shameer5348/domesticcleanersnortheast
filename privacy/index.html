Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

What we collect

When you fill in our quote form, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, postcode, the type of clean you're asking about, and anything else you choose to tell us (such as an email address or a message). If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp instead, we only see what's needed to reply to you there.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, provide a quote, and arrange a clean if you go ahead. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else for marketing purposes.

Our quote form is processed by a third-party form service, Web3Forms, which delivers your submission to our email inbox. We don't ask you to create an account or store a customer database beyond what's needed to manage your bookings.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@domesticcleanersnortheast.co.uk.

This policy is a plain-English summary of how we handle your information and isn't a substitute for professional legal advice.

Questions about your data?

Email info@domesticcleanersnortheast.co.uk and we'll get back to you.
