About Domestic Cleaners North East

A local agency connecting homes across the North East with experienced, trustworthy cleaners.

What we do

Domestic Cleaners North East is a domestic cleaning agency covering Newcastle, Sunderland, Gateshead and the surrounding towns of Tyne and Wear and Northumberland. We provide regular house cleaning, one-off deep cleans and end of tenancy cleaning for homeowners, tenants, landlords and letting agents.

Rather than a one-size-fits-all service, we match each home with a cleaner suited to the job, agree a clear price up front, and keep things flexible — there's no contract tying you in, and you can pause or cancel a regular clean whenever you need to.

Our standards

Every cleaner we send is vetted before they visit your home.

  • Experienced cleanersOur team have years of domestic cleaning behind them.
  • Fully insuredPublic liability insurance on every clean.
  • DBS-checked cleanersEvery cleaner is background checked before they visit a client's home.
  • No contractPause or cancel regular cleans whenever you need to.
