Regular house cleaning

Weekly or fortnightly cleans that keep your home on top, without you having to think about it.

What's included

Every regular clean covers the rooms that matter most day to day. Tell us what you'd like covered and we'll build your visit around it.

  • KitchenSurfaces, hob, sink, outside of appliances and floors.
  • BathroomsToilet, bath or shower, sink and floors, cleaned and disinfected.
  • Bedrooms and living areasDusting, hoovering, mopping and bins emptied.
  • Beds and laundryBed changes and light laundry, if you'd like it added.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your home

    Fill in the quote form or message us on WhatsApp with your postcode and how often you'd like a clean.

  2. Get your price

    Regular cleans start from £20 an hour. We'll confirm your price and how many hours your home typically needs.

  3. Settle into a routine

    The same cleaner visits each time where possible, on the day and time that suits you. No contract — pause or cancel whenever you need to.

Common questions

How often can I book a regular clean?

Weekly or fortnightly is most common. Let us know what suits your household and we'll build a schedule around it.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients give their cleaner a key or entry code and are out during the visit. It's entirely up to you.

Can I change what's cleaned each visit?

Yes. Let your cleaner or our team know if you'd like to add extras like inside the oven or windows for a particular visit.
