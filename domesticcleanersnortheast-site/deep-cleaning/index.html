Deep cleaning

A top-to-bottom clean that reaches the places a regular clean doesn't.

What's included

A deep clean goes further than a regular visit, working through the build-up that collects over time.

  • KitchenInside cupboards and the oven, behind and under appliances, grease and grime removed.
  • BathroomsGrout, limescale, tiles and extractor fans given proper attention.
  • Skirting boards and fixturesLight switches, door frames and skirting boards wiped down throughout.
  • Windows and sillsInternal windows and sills cleaned, inside and out on request.

Who a deep clean is for

A deep clean is a good starting point if your home needs catching up, if you're preparing for a big life event, or if you want a fresh baseline before booking regular cleans. It's also popular as a one-off spring clean.

Common questions

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the size of your home and how long it's been since it was last deep cleaned. We'll give you a time and price estimate after a quick chat.

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

No, our cleaners bring what they need. Let us know if you have a preference for particular products.

Can a deep clean lead into regular cleaning?

Yes, many clients start with a deep clean, then move straight into a weekly or fortnightly regular clean to maintain it.
