End of tenancy cleaning

A full clean of the property when you move out or move in, so it's ready for the inventory check or the next tenant.

What's included

An end of tenancy clean covers the whole property, to the standard expected for an inventory check-out.

  • Every roomKitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas cleaned throughout.
  • Kitchen detailInside the oven, fridge, cupboards and behind appliances.
  • BathroomsLimescale, grout and tiles brought back up to standard.
  • Finishing touchesSkirting boards, light switches, door frames and internal windows.

For tenants, landlords and letting agents

Whether you're a tenant preparing to hand back the keys, a landlord getting a property ready for new tenants, or a letting agent managing several properties, we work to the timescales you need. Let us know the move-out or move-in date and we'll fit around it where we can.

Common questions

Will this get my deposit back?

We clean to the standard expected for an inventory check-out, but deposit decisions are made by your landlord or agent against the original inventory. We can't guarantee the outcome, but we aim to leave the property in excellent condition.

How is this priced?

End of tenancy cleans are priced by the size and condition of the property. Tell us the number of bedrooms and we'll give you an accurate quote.

Can you work to a tight moving date?

We'll always try to fit around moving day. Get in touch as early as you can and we'll confirm our earliest availability.
